# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful, welcome different views, give and accept feedback with grace, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer through GitHub: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/danyk20/WebMirror/security) marked as a conduct report, or message [@danyk20](https://github.com/danyk20). Reports are handled privately.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close or lock threads and block users who break these rules, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
